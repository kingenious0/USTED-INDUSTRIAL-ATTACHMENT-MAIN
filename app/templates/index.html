Industrial Attachment Management System (U-IAP)

Digitizing the Workplace Experience Learning (WEL) administrative lifecycle while preserving physical institutional verification, official wet-ink company stamps, and supervisor evaluations.

Log In to U-IAP Portal

1. Liaison Intake & Verification

Mandatory physical Liaison Office checkpoint. Students are verified by University Index Number, and official Introductory Letters are generated.

2. Acceptance Form Endorsement

Host organizations complete physical acceptance forms with signatures and official wet-ink company stamps, scanned and uploaded by students.

3. Mon–Fri Logging & Locking

Expandable Monday–Friday activity log. Students manually lock each week to trigger printed sheets for physical supervisor verification.
