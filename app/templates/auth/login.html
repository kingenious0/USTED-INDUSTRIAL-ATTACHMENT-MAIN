U-IAP Practicum System Overview

The USTED Industrial Attachment Portal (U-IAP) is the official workplace experiential learning platform connecting students, university liaison officers, academic supervisors, and partner industry enterprises across the Republic of Ghana.

Key Milestones

  • Physical Walk-in Intake: Liaison officers verify Master records and issue cryptographically stamped Introductory Letters.
  • Wet-Ink Acceptance Verification: Mandatory high-resolution photo/scan audit of physical employer stamp with GPS capture.
  • Weekly eLogSheets: Daily workplace reflections strictly locked every Sunday at 23:59 GMT.
  • Zonal Supervision: Geographically clustered university faculty supervision and on-site audit visits.

Attachment Guidelines & Regulations

All students enrolled in the workplace experience learning program must adhere strictly to the USTED Practicum Handbook:

  1. Working Hours: Minimum 35 hours per week under the direct guidance of an industry workplace mentor.
  2. Acceptance Upload Deadline: Scanned acceptance letter with official physical wet-ink stamp must be submitted within 7 calendar days of placement.
  3. Logbook Integrity: Daily work logs must be truthful, concise, and submitted before the weekly locking deadline.
  4. Code of Conduct: Professional decorum, occupational safety (PPE compliance), and non-disclosure of enterprise trade secrets.

Data Privacy & Institutional Governance

U-IAP complies with the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). Student and enterprise data are handled with rigorous confidentiality:

  • Data Sovereignty: Student records and employer details remain property of USTED.
  • Audit Logging: All login attempts, document approvals, and supervisory visit entries are immutably logged with timestamps and IP addresses.
  • Security: All passwords are encrypted with salted PBKDF2/scrypt hashes. Passwords are never stored in plaintext.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my Introductory Letter? Visit the Industrial Liaison Office desk. A liaison officer will verify your student ID and print your letter with a secure verification QR code.
What is First-Time Activation? Students scanning their Introductory Letter QR code or visiting for the first time use the "First-Time Activation" tab with their Index Number and phone number to set their portal password.
What if my acceptance stamp is rejected? If a liaison officer flags your upload due to low resolution or missing wet-ink stamp, re-upload a sharp, well-lit photo showing the physical employer stamp clearly.
