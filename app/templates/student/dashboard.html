How to Begin Your Attachment

Follow the university's standard administrative procedure to start your Workplace Experience Learning:

  1. 1
    Visit the Industrial Liaison Office (Main Campus) with your Index Number
    An attachment record must be officially initiated at the Industrial Liaison Office. Present your student identity card for verification.
  2. 2
    Request your official Industrial Liaison / Introductory Letter
    Liaison staff will generate your formal Introductory Letter addressed to your prospective host enterprise.
  3. 3
    Take your Introductory Letter & Acceptance Form to your host enterprise
    Deliver the letter and blank Acceptance Form to the company for endorsement, signature, and their official wet-ink stamp.
  4. 4
    Return here to upload your signed and stamped Acceptance Form
    Once your attachment is initiated by Liaison, upload the clear scanned document to activate your weekly logbook.
Need assistance? Contact the Industrial Liaison Unit directly at the Administration Block or email the Liaison Coordinator.
