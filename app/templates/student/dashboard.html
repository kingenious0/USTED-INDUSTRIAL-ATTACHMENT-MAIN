Attachment Initialization Checklist

Follow the official USTED procedure to register your placement and generate documentation.

1
Report to Industrial Liaison Office (Main Campus)

Present your student ID card to be verified in person by a Liaison Officer. The officer will record your target organization and generate your official Introductory Letter with QR code.

2
Deliver Introductory Letter & Acceptance Form to Host Organization

Present the Introductory Letter to your workplace supervisor. Have the host organization complete the Acceptance Form, endorsing it with an official wet-ink company stamp and supervisor signature.

3
Scan Letter QR Code to Complete Onboarding & Upload Stamped Form

Scan your Introductory Letter QR code using your smartphone camera to access your portal, upload your stamped Acceptance Form, and confirm your workstation GPS location.
